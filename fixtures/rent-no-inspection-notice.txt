This Agreement is made on 10th May, 2026.

BETWEEN

Mrs. Anita Deshmukh, aged 55 years, residing at 3, Waterfield Road, Bandra West, Mumbai 400050 (hereinafter "Licensor")

AND

Mr. Rohan Gupta, aged 27 years, residing at 8, Linking Road, Khar West, Mumbai 400052 (hereinafter "Licensee")

WHEREAS the Licensor is the owner of Flat No. 204, Sapphire Heights, SV Road, Santacruz West, Mumbai 400054 ("Licensed Premises").

1. The Licensor grants the Licensee leave and license to use the Licensed Premises for 11 months commencing 1st June, 2026.

2. The Licensee shall pay a monthly license fee of Rs. 30,000/- payable on or before the 7th of each month.

3. The Licensee shall not sublet or assign the Licensed Premises.

4. Rent Escalation
4.1 The license fee shall increase by 10% upon renewal after 11 months.

5. Lock-in Period
5.1 A lock-in period of 5 months applies.
5.2 Early exit during lock-in forfeits the security deposit.

6. The Licensor shall be entitled to visit and inspect the Licensed Premises at any time and for any reason. The Licensee shall ensure the premises are accessible for the Licensor at all times.

7. The Licensee shall deposit a security deposit of Rs. 1,20,000/- at the time of signing. The deposit shall be refunded within 30 days of handing over vacant possession, less any deductions for damage or dues.

8. The Licensee shall maintain the premises properly. All repairs, whether minor or structural, shall be borne by the Licensee.

9. Termination
9.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement with 30 days written notice after lock-in.
9.2 The Licensor may terminate immediately if any term is breached.

10. The premises shall be used for residential purposes only.

11. Electricity, water, and internet are the Licensee's responsibility. Society maintenance is the Licensor's.

12. This Agreement is governed by the laws of India with exclusive jurisdiction of Mumbai courts.
