Ms. Priya Mehta, aged about 26 years, residing at 45, Carter Road, Bandra West, Mumbai 400050, Maharashtra, India, PAN: ABCDE1234F, Aadhaar: 1234 5678 9012 (hereinafter referred to as the "Licensee" which expression shall mean and include her heirs, successors, legal representatives and assigns) of the OTHER PART.

WHEREAS the Licensor is the absolute owner of Flat No. 302, 3rd Floor, Seaview Apartments, Plot No. 78, Linking Road, Khar West, Mumbai 400052, Maharashtra, India (hereinafter referred to as the "Licensed Premises").

AND WHEREAS the Licensee has approached the Licensor and requested leave and license to use and occupy the Licensed Premises for residential purposes and the Licensor has agreed to grant the same on the terms and conditions hereinafter mentioned.

NOW THEREFORE THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSETH AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Licensor hereby grants to the Licensee leave and license to use and occupy the Licensed Premises for a period of 11 (Eleven) months commencing from the 1st day of April, 2026 and ending on the 28th day of February, 2027.

2. The Licensee shall pay to the Licensor a monthly license fee of Rs. 35,000/- (Rupees Thirty-Five Thousand Only) payable on or before the 5th day of each calendar month. In case of delay beyond 10 days, a late fee of Rs. 500/- per day shall be charged.

3. The Licensee shall not sublet, assign, or transfer the Licensed Premises or any part thereof to any third party without the prior written consent of the Licensor.

4. Rent Escalation and Revision

4.1 The license fee shall be subject to an increase of 10% at the time of renewal.

4.2 The Licensor reserves the right to increase the license fee by up to 10% after the completion of 11 months, upon giving 30 days written notice to the Licensee.

5. Lock-in Period

5.1 The Licensee agrees to a lock-in period of 6 (Six) months from the date of commencement of this Agreement.

5.2 During the lock-in period, the Licensee shall not be entitled to terminate this Agreement. If the Licensee chooses to vacate during the lock-in period, the Licensee shall forfeit the entire security deposit.

6. The Licensee shall maintain the Licensed Premises in good and tenantable condition and shall not make any structural alterations without the prior written consent of the Licensor. Any damage caused beyond normal wear and tear shall be repaired at the cost of the Licensee. The determination of what constitutes normal wear and tear shall be at the sole discretion of the Licensor.

7. The Licensee shall deposit an interest-free security deposit of Rs. 1,50,000/- (Rupees One Lakh Fifty Thousand Only) with the Licensor, to be paid at the time of execution of this Agreement. The security deposit may be adjusted against any damages, unpaid license fees, or other dues at the sole discretion of the Licensor.

8. The Licensor shall be entitled to enter and inspect the Licensed Premises at any time without prior notice to ensure that the Licensee is maintaining the premises properly and complying with the terms of this Agreement.

9. Termination

9.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving 30 days written notice to the other party, subject to the lock-in period in Clause 5.

9.2 The Licensor may terminate this Agreement immediately and without notice if the Licensee breaches any term or condition of this Agreement.

9.3 Upon termination, the Licensee shall hand over vacant and peaceful possession of the Licensed Premises to the Licensor on or before the date of termination.

10. The Licensee shall use the Licensed Premises solely for residential purposes and shall not use the same for any commercial, illegal, or immoral purposes.

11. Society Charges and Utilities

11.1 The Licensee shall bear and pay all charges for electricity, water, gas, and internet used in the Licensed Premises during the term of the Agreement.

11.2 The society maintenance charges shall be borne by the Licensor.

12. The Licensee shall abide by all rules and regulations of the housing society and shall not do anything that causes nuisance or annoyance to other residents.

13. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India. The courts of Mumbai shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.

14. This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements, negotiations, and discussions.
