LEAVE AND LICENSE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on the 1st day of June, 2026.

BETWEEN

Mr. Suresh Rao, aged 48 years, residing at 5, Turner Road, Bandra West, Mumbai 400050 (hereinafter "Licensor")

AND

Mr. Vikram Patel, aged 29 years, residing at 12, Hill Road, Bandra West, Mumbai 400050 (hereinafter "Licensee")

WHEREAS the Licensor is the owner of Flat No. 501, Horizon Towers, SV Road, Khar West, Mumbai 400052 ("Licensed Premises").

1. The Licensor grants the Licensee leave and license to use and occupy the Licensed Premises for 11 months commencing 1st July, 2026.

2. The Licensee shall pay a monthly license fee of Rs. 28,000/- payable on or before the 5th of each month.

3. The Licensee shall not sublet or assign the Licensed Premises without prior written consent.

4. Rent Escalation
4.1 The license fee shall increase by 8% upon renewal.

5. Lock-in Period
5.1 The Licensee agrees to a lock-in period of 4 months.
5.2 Early termination during lock-in results in forfeiture of deposit.

6. The Licensee shall maintain the premises in good condition. Structural repairs are the Licensor's responsibility. Minor repairs below Rs. 2,000 are the Licensee's responsibility.

7. The Licensee shall deposit a security deposit of Rs. 1,00,000/- at the time of signing.

8. The Licensor may inspect the premises with reasonable prior written notice during business hours.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement with 30 days written notice after lock-in.

10. The Licensee shall use the premises solely for residential purposes.

11. Society maintenance charges shall be borne by the Licensor. Electricity, water, gas, and internet shall be borne by the Licensee.

12. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India. Courts of Mumbai shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
